How final review works

  1. A final review is available after a reevaluation results in a second denial.6 CAR § 35-113(a)(2)(A)
  2. Submit within business days of the date you were told the reevaluation was denied. Late requests are not accepted.6 CAR § 35-113(a)(2)(B)
  3. The final approval or denial is made with the approval of the assistant commissioner or director overseeing the program.6 CAR § 35-113(a)(2)(C)
  4. You will receive the final determination in writing.6 CAR § 35-113(a)(2)(D)

Business days exclude weekends and Arkansas state holidays.
