How reevaluation works

  1. Submit within business days of the date your rejection notice was sent. Late requests are not accepted.6 CAR § 35-113(a)(1)(C)
  2. Include new documentation or clarifying information that answers the reason for the denial.6 CAR § 35-113(a)(1)(B)
  3. If the expense is not a core educational expense, you may be asked for more information first.6 CAR § 35-113(a)(1)(D)
  4. The reevaluation is conducted in consultation with a supervisor.6 CAR § 35-113(a)(1)(E)
  5. If it is denied again, you may request a final review within business days of that notice. The final decision is made in writing with the approval of the assistant commissioner or program director.6 CAR § 35-113(a)(2)(B)-(D)

Business days exclude weekends and Arkansas state holidays.
